🌍 The Solar System

Exploring Our Cosmic Neighborhood

☀️

Overview

  • 8 Planets orbiting the Sun
  • Located in the Milky Way Galaxy
  • Age: ~4.6 billion years
  • Sun contains 99.86% of total mass

☀️ The Sun

Type: G-type main-sequence star

Diameter: 1.39 million km

Temperature: 5,500°C (surface)

Age: 4.6 billion years

Composition: 73% Hydrogen, 25% Helium

🔴 Mercury

Distance from Sun: 57.9 million km

Diameter: 4,879 km

Day Duration: 59 Earth days

Atmosphere: Virtually none

Key Feature: Fastest planet, extreme temperatures

🟡 Venus

Distance from Sun: 108.2 million km

Diameter: 12,104 km

Day Duration: 243 Earth days

Temperature: 465°C (hottest planet)

Key Feature: Rotates backward, thick CO₂ atmosphere

🌍 Earth

Distance from Sun: 149.6 million km

Diameter: 12,742 km

Day Duration: 24 hours

Temperature: -88°C to 58°C

Key Feature: Only known planet with life, 1 Moon

🔴 Mars

Distance from Sun: 227.9 million km

Diameter: 6,779 km

Day Duration: 24.6 hours

Temperature: -87°C to -5°C

Key Feature: Red planet, 2 moons, active rovers

🟠 Jupiter

Distance from Sun: 778.5 million km

Diameter: 139,820 km

Day Duration: 10 hours

Moons: 95+

Key Feature: Largest planet, Great Red Spot storm

💛 Saturn

Distance from Sun: 1.43 billion km

Diameter: 116,460 km

Day Duration: 10.7 hours

Moons: 146+

Key Feature: Magnificent ring system

🔵 Uranus

Distance from Sun: 2.87 billion km

Diameter: 50,724 km

Day Duration: 17 hours

Moons: 28+

Key Feature: Rotates on its side, ice giant

🔷 Neptune

Distance from Sun: 4.50 billion km

Diameter: 49,244 km

Day Duration: 16 hours

Moons: 16+

Key Feature: Farthest planet, fastest winds in solar system

Key Takeaways

  • ⭐ Sun is the center and dominates the system
  • 🪨 Inner planets are rocky; outer are gas/ice giants
  • 🌍 Earth is unique - the only known habitable planet
  • 🔭 Constantly being studied and explored
  • 🚀 Future: Human exploration to Mars and beyond
